# Royal Packaging CRM Design System

The interface is a light-first precision logistics control room. It treats dense operational information with warm paper and board neutrals, a royal-indigo action accent, restrained borders, compact status chips, and deliberate monospace treatment for identifiers and quantities.

Typography uses IBM Plex Sans for the working UI, DM Serif Display for brand-level headings, and IBM Plex Mono for operational numerals. The component language is compact but breathable, with 6px to 14px radii, visible keyboard focus, and motion limited to 120ms, 180ms, and 300ms transitions.

The shell supports a dark theme through the same semantic tokens. Screens remain honest about Phase 1 scope: route shells may show clearly labeled demo data, while future workflows are represented as structure and empty states rather than simulated business behavior.
